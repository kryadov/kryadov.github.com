# Variations as a Service

Everybody knows the three flavours of "as a service": IaaS, PaaS and SaaS. But there are also:

1\. **BaaS (Backup as a Service)**: cloud backup of any resource (files, databases, VMs) plus storage outside the customer's site.

![Backup as a Service](/assets/blog/variations-as-a-service/03.png)

2\. **DRaaS (Disaster Recovery as a Service)**: a cloud service that restores servers to working order after a disaster.

![Disaster Recovery as a Service](/assets/blog/variations-as-a-service/04.png)

3\. **BaaS (Backend as a Service)**: an approach that lets web and mobile developers connect their apps to cloud storage and server-side APIs, along with user management, notifications and social network integration. Also known as mBaaS, mobile backend as a service.

![Backend as a Service](/assets/blog/variations-as-a-service/05.png)

4\. **DaaS (Desktop as a Service)**: access to a ready-to-use remote desktop running in the cloud.

5\. **VaaS (Video as a Service)**: a model for delivering and exchanging video content through a cloud provider.

![Video as a Service](/assets/blog/variations-as-a-service/07.jpg)

6\. **MaaS (Monitoring as a Service)**: monitoring the customer's resources from the cloud.

7\. **MaaS (Metal as a Service)** / **HaaS (Hardware as a Service)**: hardware provided in the cloud / automated operating system installation.

![Metal as a Service](/assets/blog/variations-as-a-service/09.png)

8\. **DBaaS (Database as a Service)**: database instances provided in the cloud.

![Database as a Service](/assets/blog/variations-as-a-service/10.png)

9\. **SecaaS (Security as a Service)**: security services delivered from the cloud (web access, URL filtering and so on).

![Security as a Service](/assets/blog/variations-as-a-service/11.png)

10\. **AIaaS (Artificial Intelligence as a Service)**: AI services in the cloud (speech, text, image and video recognition, speech and text translation, recommender systems and so on).

11\. **CaaS (Communications as a Service)**: communications as a service, essentially an enterprise communications solution: voice over VoIP networks, instant messaging or video conferencing.

![Communications as a Service](/assets/blog/variations-as-a-service/13.png)

12\. **STaaS (Storage as a Service)**: storage as a service: disk space on demand.

![Storage as a Service](/assets/blog/variations-as-a-service/14.jpg)

13\. **NaaS (Network as a Service)**: the network as a service.

![Network as a Service](/assets/blog/variations-as-a-service/15.png)
