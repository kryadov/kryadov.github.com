# IBM buys Red Hat, Red Hat suddenly ships the RHEL 8 Beta

The sequence of events:

1. On 28 October 2018 IBM and Red Hat [signed](https://web.archive.org/web/20200101012945/https://newsroom.ibm.com/2018-10-28-IBM-To-Acquire-Red-Hat-Completely-Changing-The-Cloud-Landscape-And-Becoming-Worlds-1-Hybrid-Cloud-Provider) the agreement to merge. [Proof](https://www.redhat.com/en/about/press-releases/ibm-acquire-red-hat-completely-changing-cloud-landscape-and-becoming-worlds-1-hybrid-cloud-provider?intcmp=701f2000000RWK2AAO).
2. On 15 November 2018 Red Hat Enterprise Linux 8 Beta "suddenly" comes out. [Proof](https://developers.redhat.com/blog/2018/11/15/red-hat-enterprise-linux-8-beta-is-here/).

In my opinion, point 2 is Red Hat trying to get done whatever it still can before the new rules of the game arrive.
In that light, I am very curious about what becomes of [OpenShift](https://www.openshift.com), [Ansible](https://www.ansible.com) and [JBoss Middleware](https://www.redhat.com/en/store/jboss-middleware).
